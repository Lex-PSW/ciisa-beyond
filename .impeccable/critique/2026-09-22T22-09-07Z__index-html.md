## Overall Impression

Hero (top) and registration (bottom) are the strongest and weakest ends of the same funnel. The one action the page exists for has no error handling, no deadline near the ask, no reassurance. The agenda mid-section is the busiest part relative to what it communicates.

## What's Working

1. Lottie wordmark reveal + continuous "O" spin — bespoke motion via custom CSS/JS handoff.
2. Fixed hero video reappearing behind registration — cheap, smart continuity device.
3. Brand gradient discipline — exact 3-stop order enforced across 7+ declarations with zero drift.

## Priority Issues

[P0] No fallback if the Microsoft Forms iframe fails to load. Why: RSVP count (the top metric) routes through one iframe with zero error state. Fix: visible fallback link/email + timeout message. Command: /impeccable harden

[P1] Registration deadline invisible until the very bottom. Why: referrals landing via hero/nav CTA may never see the Oct 16 cutoff. Fix: surface a deadline chip near the top CTA too. Command: /impeccable clarify

[P1] Agenda section overloads motion without adding comprehension. Why: SVG wave + scanner dot + dashed pulse + 6 marker dots all animate concurrently for a 6-item list already duplicated as mobile cards; cognitive-load check failed chunking, one-thing-at-a-time, progressive disclosure. Fix: cut to 1-2 concurrent animations or trigger once on scroll-into-view. Command: /impeccable quieter

[P2] Eight instances of sub-11px functional text in the agenda (9-9.5px: Registro, Clausura, 10:00 AM, Networking, Casos de éxito, Rifas/Sorpresas, 7:00 PM). Fix: raise to >=11-12px. Command: /impeccable typeset

[P2] Venue photo alt text asserts a false fact ("Fachada de El Ejecutivo Eventos" on images that show the old Luum venue) — compounds the already-tracked stale-photo gap for screen-reader users. Fix: neutral alt text until real photos exist. Command: /impeccable audit
